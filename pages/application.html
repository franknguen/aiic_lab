Application information

Join us

The lab takes new MS, Ph.D. and joint MS/Ph.D. students every semester. Admission is through the Graduate School of Sejong University; the lab itself provides the funding. Write to Prof. Kim first.

1. Benefits for new members

No tuition fee
Salary
Two incentives

Detailed information: email Prof. Kim at ks@sejong.ac.kr.

2. Website for application

Sejong University — Graduate School (international admissions)

3. English certificate

  • If candidates submit an English certificate before the application deadline, they do not need to submit another one later and can receive their degree.
  • If candidates cannot submit an English test result before the deadline, they must submit an additional document: a “Letter of Verification of Research Capability” signed by both the professor and the Dean.
  • In that case the candidate must still submit a valid English certificate later in order to graduate and receive the degree.

4. Department

Department of Artificial Intelligence and Information Technology, Sejong University, Seoul, Korea.

5. What to put in your first email

A short CV, your transcript, the topic you would like to work on (see the research fields), and any English test score you already have. Mentioning a paper of the lab you have read helps.

Note

Sections 1–4 are the lab's rules; section 5 is practical advice.
