AIIC

AI & Intelligent Communication Lab

The AIIC Lab is led by Prof. Sunghwan Kim at the Department of Artificial Intelligence and Information Technology, Sejong University (Seoul, Korea). We study next-generation wireless communications, error-correcting codes for DNA data storage, and quantum information — the mathematics of sending and keeping information reliably, from radio channels to DNA molecules and qubits.

Research fields

Three main topics, with AI and machine learning running through all of them. Click a card for the detailed page where one exists.

Next-generation communication technology

  • Non-orthogonal multiple access (NOMA), rate-splitting multiple access (RSMA)
  • Reconfigurable intelligent surfaces (RIS)
  • 5G/6G new radio access, OFDM, massive MIMO and its energy efficiency
  • Short-packet / URLLC, UAV and satellite links, visible light communication
Read more

DNA storage

  • Error-correcting codes for data recovery
  • Insertion/deletion codes under GC-content and homopolymer-run constraints
  • LDPC code design and LLR calculation for the DNA channel
  • Databases on DNA
Read more

Quantum information

  • Quantum computing
  • Quantum error-correcting codes: stabilizer codes, additive codes over GF(4)
  • Quantum protocols: three-pass protocol, counterfactual secret sharing
See publications

AI-based communication & machine learning

  • Transformer-based error-correction decoders
  • Semantic communication, federated learning
  • TinyML and large models for 6G, quantum reinforcement learning
